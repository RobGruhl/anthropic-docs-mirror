# Change your Team plan from monthly to annual billing

*Updated over 5 months ago*

---

Owners and Primary Owners of Team plans with monthly subscriptions can switch from the monthly plan to an annual plan by following these steps:

1. Navigate to **[Organization settings > Billing](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/billing)** or **[claude.ai/upgrade](http://claude.ai/upgrade)**.
2. You’ll see a banner confirming your current monthly plan in your Billing settings; click the “Switch to Annual” button.
3. Or from /upgrade, click the “Switch to Annual plan” button:
3.  
3. <img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/lupk8zyo/1690325734/d47f714680d78408d6022d06b8d1/image.png?expires=1785780000&signature=cf5f68d88948a2d9e56db1687023f45e6e82a20ef66a6551fd0310df22217313&req=dSYuFsp8mIZcXfMW3Hu4gZzas%2FXtvDhanWrRiVwqPzarFpozCQJGo8EzmbKJ%0A0A%3D%3D%0A" width="450" alt="">
3.  
4. The confirmation screen will display the total cost for your upgrade from monthly to annual billing:
4.  
4. <img src="https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/lupk8zyo/1690326039/3a91cdc5fff57d188a18ecc6273f/image.png?expires=1785780000&signature=04a2b17e92499e41a54ab11e738d07f9fa8c0205ca1ef428e18f21caf01cd4ad&req=dSYuFsp8m4FcUPMW3Hu4gbNj%2Bk78WQrpi%2B5vzcg6znVkwboS%2B%2Bq8%2Fn2%2FznjZ%0AqA%3D%3D%0A" width="350" alt="">
4.  
5. Click “Confirm subscription.”
6. Your new annual Team plan will start immediately and you will be credited for any unused time on your previous monthly plan.
